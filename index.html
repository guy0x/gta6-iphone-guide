Hidden controls

PlayStation / Xbox notation follows the source. Context matters: vehicle, weapon, mission, or character state can affect availability.

Vehicle • 01

Leave car engine running

When exiting, double-tap Triangle / Y to keep the engine on.

Vehicle • 02

Leave boat engine running

Double-tap Triangle / Y in a boat; it continues straight while you move around the deck.

Vehicle • 03

Auto Drive

Hold D-pad Down + Options / Menu for cinematic camera, then hold Circle / B to let Jason or Lucia drive to the selected destination.

Vehicle • 04

Vehicle options

Hold D-pad Left for the radio menu, then flick the Right Stick for lights, windows, convertible roof, or context options such as a boat anchor.

Vehicle • 05

Flip your car

When completely overturned, press L3 / LS, then hold Square / X.

Vehicle • 06

Push a jet ski into water

Stand behind it and hold R1 / RB.

Vehicle • 07

Use your door as a weapon

In a car, tap Square / X to strike with the door. On a bike, this becomes a kick.

Vehicle • 08

Duck and hide

In the driver’s seat, hold Circle / B to duck low.

Vehicle • 09–11

Signals, road rage, radio

D-pad Left / Right for turn signals; R3 / RS to yell; double-tap D-pad Left for radio on/off.

Vehicle • 12–13

Partner drive, wheelie, stoppie

With both protagonists by a car: hold D-pad Right and select Cross / A to hand over. While accelerating, pull Left Stick back for a wheelie or forward for a stoppie.

On foot • 14–16

Hood slide, roll, rest

Sprint toward a hood + Cross / A to slide. While aiming, steer with Left Stick + Cross / A to combat roll. R3 / RS leans by a wall; hold Circle / B to sit where available.

On foot • 17–19

Inspect, ADS, shoulder swap

Hold Left Trigger + press Right Stick for Inspect Mode. While aiming a weapon with a suitable optic, press R3 / RS to ADS. Tap D-pad Left to switch shoulder.

On foot • 20–23

Chokehold, gesture, cigarette, calls

Grapple with R2, hold then repeatedly press Square / X for a chokehold. With no weapon: target NPC with Left Trigger + hold R1 to gesture. While smoking, Left Trigger + Right Trigger flicks a cigarette. Circle / B rejects calls.

Community mystery lead

Unverified • major spoilers

“Life Is Passing Me By” / Vice City lead

A video by Memeio claims an easter-egg route leads to a Vice City sequence and rewards. This guide records the lead—not a confirmed fact. Use a separate save before testing.

Show the claimed route
  1. Go to the radio tower in Key Lento, climb upstairs, and inspect the poster room. The video says a power switch and button trigger a Vice City sound.
  2. Find 12 LPL electrical substations across the map and collect the wall letters. The video claims they spell “Life Is Passing Me By.”
  3. The video links that phrase to a Vice City cheat-code reference and claims the tower poster provides the next clue.
  4. It then claims that entering an input sequence across the substations—without fast travel—triggers a message directing the player to Reactor 2 during an evening/night window.
  5. The video claims Reactor 2 is at the LPL Turtle Head power station and that a portal appears after 7 p.m.
  6. Claimed outcome: a Vice City sequence plus a Tommy Vercetti shirt and Malibu bike. Verify each step yourself; this guide intentionally does not present the claim as established game fact.

Source video: Memeio on X (23 Nov 2026). Its on-screen “Lose Track of Time / Life Is Passing Me By” clue was visually reviewed; the broader route and rewards remain unverified here.
